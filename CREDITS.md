# Credits

Moo would not exist without Miguel de Icaza. It is a fork of his terminal,
running on his terminal engine, and nearly every line of it is his work.

## Tecolot

**Moo is a fork of [Tecolot](https://github.com/migueldeicaza/Tecolot)**, a
native macOS terminal by [Miguel de Icaza](https://github.com/migueldeicaza).
Tecolot is the application — the window and pane management, the profiles and
themes, the AppleScript and App Intents automation, the session persistence.
Moo changes a small part of it and renames the result; the rest is Tecolot.

Copyright © 2026 Miguel de Icaza. MIT License.

## SwiftTerm

The terminal emulation itself — parsing, buffers, rendering, the pty — is
[SwiftTerm](https://github.com/migueldeicaza/SwiftTerm), also by Miguel de
Icaza. It is the engine this program is built on.

SwiftTerm in turn builds on the work of the
[xterm.js](https://github.com/xtermjs/xterm.js) authors, SourceLair Private
Company, and Christopher Jeffrey.

Copyright © Miguel de Icaza. MIT License.
